Safety

Merchant boundaries

Every auto-action is capped. Above threshold → escalate. Fraud never auto-contests.

Capture Guardian

  • Auto-act max: ₹50,000 (above this → ask a human)
  • Min age: 6 hours

Retry Strategist

  • Max retries: 2
  • Retry gap: 30 min
  • Min amount: ₹100

Dispute Defender

  • Auto-contest max: ₹25,000
  • Min win chance: 0.6 (how likely we can win the dispute)
  • Never auto: fraud

Refund Resolver

  • Auto-reissue max: ₹10,000
  • Min pending age: 7 days

Checkout Rescuer

  • Min order: ₹500
  • Wait before send: 30 min
  • Give up after: 24 hours
  • Max messages: 1
